What happens in your first Malayalam tutor lesson?

A first Malayalam tutor lesson should make you feel clearer, not embarrassed. The best first class finds your goal, your level and the exact practice path you need.

See Malayalam tutor support

A good first lesson flow

Goal check. The tutor asks why you want Malayalam: family, spouse, travel, work, kids, school or culture.
Sound check. You try a few beginner words so pronunciation can be corrected early.
Starter phrases. You practise greetings, introductions and one daily-use conversation pattern.
Correction style. The tutor shows how feedback will work without stopping every sentence.
Homework. You leave with a small speaking task, not a huge grammar assignment.

What to prepare before class

Your reason

Write one sentence about why Malayalam matters to you now.

Your comfort level

Say whether you can understand, read, speak or only recognize a few words.

Your first scene

Choose one situation: family call, food order, travel question, child practice or workplace need.
