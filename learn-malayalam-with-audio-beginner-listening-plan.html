Learn Malayalam with audio: beginner listening plan

Malayalam becomes easier when you hear the rhythm daily. A simple listening plan can train your ear before grammar starts feeling heavy.

Open pronunciation guide

Daily 15-minute audio routine

Minute 1-3: Listen once without pausing. Let the sound pattern become familiar.
Minute 4-7: Replay and repeat short phrases after the speaker.
Minute 8-11: Shadow the audio quietly, matching rhythm and pauses.
Minute 12-15: Record your version and mark one sound to improve tomorrow.

What beginners should hear first

Greetings

Hello, welcome, how are you, thank you and polite replies.

Family words

Amma, achan, chechi, chettan and family-call patterns.

Daily requests

Water, tea, food, help, directions, repeat please and I do not understand.
