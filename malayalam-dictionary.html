A learner's dictionary of everyday Malayalam

This is a short dictionary of the words my beginners use most, each with the Malayalam script, a simple romanisation and a real example sentence. It is deliberately small: knowing these 48 words well, and in sentences, is worth more than recognising a thousand from a list.

Verbs are listed in their dictionary form (ending in -uka), with an example of how they are actually spoken.

Question words

MalayalamRomanisedMeaningExample
എന്ത്enthuwhatithu enthaanu? What is this?
എവിടെevidewhereamma evide? Where is mother?
ആര്aaruwhoavide aaraanu? Who is there?
എപ്പോൾeppolwheneppol varum? When will you come?
എങ്ങനെenganehowavide engane pokum? How do I get there?
എത്രethrahow much / manyithinu ethrayaanu? How much is this?
എന്തുകൊണ്ട്enthukonduwhyenthukondu vannilla? Why didn't you come?
ഏത്ethuwhichethu bus? Which bus?
