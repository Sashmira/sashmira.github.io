Weekend Malayalam school or one-to-one lessons? An honest comparison for parents abroad

By Reshmi R Nair, Malayalam Linguist · Updated October 2026

If you live abroad and want your child to learn Malayalam, you will usually choose between a weekend Malayalam school run by the community and one-to-one lessons online. Both are good; they do different jobs. Here is an honest comparison to help you choose, or combine them.

The two options

Weekend schools are usually run by Malayali associations, community organisations or Malayalam Mission learning centres, often with volunteer teachers. Malayalam Mission, a Kerala government programme, offers a staged course (Kanikkonna, Sooryakanthi, Aambal and Neelakurinji) through such centres in many countries.

One-to-one lessons are taught online by a single teacher, built around one child's level, family and goals, at a time that suits your time zone.

When the weekend school is the right answer

  • Your child enjoys groups and would love Malayali friends.
  • You want culture, festivals and performances to be part of it.
  • There is a well-run centre within reach.

When one-to-one is the right answer

  • Your child understands Malayalam but won't speak it, and needs a low-pressure space.
  • There is no school nearby, or the schedule doesn't fit.
  • You want faster progress on a specific goal, such as speaking with grandparents or reading the script.

What many families do

Many families combine the two: the weekend school for community, friendships and culture, and a short one-to-one lesson during the week for speaking confidence. The two support each other rather than compete.

Frequently asked questions

At what age should a child start?

For speaking, the earlier the better, at home. Weekend schools often start around five or six; one-to-one lessons can start once a child can sit for a short session, usually around five.

Will my child learn to read and write at a weekend school?

Many weekend schools teach the script, often following the Malayalam Mission stages. Ask the centre about their syllabus.

How do I find a weekend school near me?

Ask your local Malayali association or community organisation, or look for a Malayalam Mission learning centre in your country.
