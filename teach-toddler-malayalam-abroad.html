Teaching a toddler Malayalam abroad: a 10-minute daily routine (ages 1 to 4)

By Reshmi R Nair, Malayalam Linguist · Updated October 2026

Toddlers learn language through repetition, play and routine, not lessons. Ten minutes a day of the same Malayalam, at the same moments, does more than an hour on Saturday. Here is a routine by age, with the exact words.

Three principles

  • Same words, same moments. Use the same Malayalam at bath time, meals and bedtime every day.
  • Answer, don't test. If your toddler answers in English, reply naturally in Malayalam rather than asking them to repeat.
  • Make Malayalam useful. The words that get them things (food, a toy, a hug) are the ones they learn first.

Ages 1 to 2: naming

Point and name, many times a day. Ask "where is it?" and let them point.

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
ഇത് എന്താ?ithu enthaa?What is this?
പന്ത് എവിടെ?panthu evide?Where is the ball?
ഇതാ പൂച്ചithaa poochaHere's the cat
കണ്ണ് എവിടെ?kannu evide?Where's your eye?
ഉമ്മ താumma thaaGive me a kiss

Ages 2 to 3: simple instructions

Short commands they can act on turn Malayalam into a game.

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
വാvaaCome
ഇരിക്ക്irikkuSit
ഇത് താithu thaaGive me this
കൈ കഴുകാംkai kazhukaamLet's wash hands
ഉടുപ്പ് ഇടാംuduppu idaamLet's put your clothes on

Ages 3 to 4: questions and choices

Offer choices in Malayalam so they have to use a word to answer.

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
പാല് വേണോ, വെള്ളം വേണോ?paalu veno, vellam veno?Milk or water?
എന്താ വേണ്ടേ?enthaa vende?What do you want?
ആരാ ഇത്?aaraa ithu?Who is this?
നമുക്ക് കളിക്കാംnammukku kalikkaamLet's play
ഇന്ന് എന്താ ചെയ്തേ?innu enthaa cheythe?What did you do today?

The ten-minute routine

  1. Two minutes at breakfast: name everything on the table.
  2. Three minutes of a picture book: any picture book works; describe the pictures in Malayalam instead of reading the English text.
  3. Two minutes of a song: the same Malayalam rhyme or song every day.
  4. Three minutes at bedtime: "where is...?" with body parts and toys, then umma and chaachikko.

What to expect

Understanding comes first, often for months, before speaking. Many toddlers mix Malayalam and English words in one sentence; that is normal and a good sign. Keep going, and keep it warm.

Mixed families: if only one parent speaks Malayalam, that parent keeps the routine and the other parent learns the same handful of words, so Malayalam belongs to the whole household.

Frequently asked questions

My toddler only answers in English. Should I insist?

No. Reply in Malayalam and keep the routine; insisting turns the language into a test. Most children start answering once Malayalam is clearly useful at home.

Are videos enough?

Videos help with songs, but toddlers learn language mainly from people talking with them. Use videos as a small extra, not the main source.

Should I teach the script to a toddler?

Not yet. Speaking and listening come first; the script can wait until five or six.
