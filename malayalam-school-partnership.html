What we can offer

A spoken-Malayalam syllabus in levels

A structured path built on the RRN Method: sound before script, spoken before textbook, and RRN Lego-Block sentence structures that learners can reuse straight away.

Teacher-support sessions

Online sessions for the people who teach your class. Volunteer teachers get a free first session, so you can see whether it helps before going further.

Free 30-day course for families

Families can use SpeakMalayalam.com's free 30-day course at home between classes.

A teacher's reference book

Speak Malayalam: The Complete Colloquial Course on Google Play Books is a reference for teachers planning lessons.

Proficiency certificate

A SpeakMalayalam proficiency certificate is in development. It is not available yet, and we will say so plainly until it is.

About Reshmi R Nair

Reshmi R Nair is an applied linguist with 15+ years of experience teaching native English speakers and the Malayali diaspora worldwide, and 3,000+ students in 10+ countries. She founded SpeakMalayalam and wrote Speak Malayalam: The Complete Colloquial Course.
