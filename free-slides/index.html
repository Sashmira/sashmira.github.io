Frequently asked questions

Are the slides really free?

Yes. Every lesson’s slides are free to download for personal study. The full course adds quizzes, a workbook, cheat sheets and a certificate.

Which level should I start at?

If you are new to Malayalam, start at lesson 1. If you can already hold a simple conversation, go to the B1 lessons. Not sure? Take the free level test.

Do I need to read the Malayalam script?

No. Every slide shows Malayalam in script, in English letters and in English, so you can learn to speak first and pick up the script along the way.
