Malayalam Opposites: Big & Small, Hot & Cold, Right & Wrong

By Reshmi R Nair, Malayalam Linguist · Published October 2026

Describing words are easiest to learn in pairs: big and small, hot and cold, right and wrong, good and bad. This guide gives you the most useful Malayalam opposites, how they change before a noun, and the everyday sentences you’ll use them in. Each word comes with the Malayalam script, an easy romanised spelling and the English meaning.

Big and small

On their own, “big” and “small” are valuthu and cheruthu. Before a noun they become valiya and cheriya: valiya veedu (a big house), cheriya kutty (a little child).

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
വലുത്ValuthuBig (one / a big one)
ചെറുത്CheruthuSmall (one / a small one)
വലിയValiyaBig (before a noun)
ചെറിയCheriyaSmall (before a noun)
ഇതിന്റെ വലിയ സൈസ് ഉണ്ടോ?Ithinte valiya size undo?Do you have this in a bigger size?
ഒരു ചെറിയ കപ്പ് മതിOru cheriya cup mathiA small cup is enough

Hot and cold

Choodu (heat) and thanuppu (cold) work for weather, food and water alike. Kerala is hot most of the year, so nalla choodaa! is a very common complaint.

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
ചൂട്ChooduHeat / hot
തണുപ്പ്ThanuppuCold / coldness
നല്ല ചൂടാNalla choodaaIt’s really hot
നല്ല തണുപ്പുണ്ട്Nalla thanuppunduIt’s quite cold
ചൂടുവെള്ളം / തണുത്ത വെള്ളംChooduvellam / Thanutha vellamHot water / Cold water
വെള്ളം ചൂടാണോ?Vellam choodaano?Is the water hot?

Correct and wrong

Sheri means correct, okay and alright all in one. Thettu means wrong, or a mistake.

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
ശരിSheriCorrect / right / okay
തെറ്റ്ThettuWrong / a mistake
ഇത് ശരിയാണോ?Ithu shariyaano?Is this right?
അതെ, ശരിയാAthe, shariyaaYes, it’s right
അത് തെറ്റാAthu thettaaThat’s wrong
എന്റെ തെറ്റാ, ക്ഷമിക്കണംEnte thettaa, kshamikkanamMy mistake, sorry

Good and bad

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish

നല്ല കുട്ടിNalla kuttyA good child

How describing words change before a noun

Many describing words have two forms. The short form goes before a noun (valiya, cheriya, nalla, puthiya); the form ending in -thu stands alone or ends a sentence (valuthu, cheruthu, nallathu, puthiyathu). So you say puthiya phone (a new phone) but ithu puthiyathaa (this one is new).

Frequently asked questions

How do you say big and small in Malayalam?

Valuthu (big) and cheruthu (small). Before a noun: valiya and cheriya, as in valiya veedu (big house).

How do you say hot and cold in Malayalam?

Choodu (heat, hot) and thanuppu (cold). “It’s really hot” is “nalla choodaa”.

How do you say correct and wrong in Malayalam?

Sheri (correct, okay) and thettu (wrong, mistake). “Is this right?” is “ithu shariyaano?”

How do you say good and bad in Malayalam?

Nalla / nallathu (good) and cheetha (bad).
