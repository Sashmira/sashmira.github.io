chechielder sisterChechi veettil undo?Is elder sister at home?
chettanelder brotherChettan evide aanu?Where is elder brother?
aniyanyounger brotherAniyan schoolil aanu.Younger brother is at school.
aniyathiyounger sisterAniyathi padikkunnu.Younger sister is studying.
ammummagrandmotherAmmumma sukhamano?Is grandmother well?
