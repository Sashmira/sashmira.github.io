German is the surprise here — four cases, verb-final subordinate clauses, and a true dative subject. Mir ist kalt is enikku thanuppundu with German words.

Shared grammatical features with Malayalam

Each language scored against the same ten features used throughout this site. A full match counts 1, a partial match 0.5.

German 5 / 10

The strongest European match. Four cases, verb-final subordinate clauses, du/Sie — and a true dative subject: mir ist kalt is enikku thanuppundu exactly.

Indonesian 3 / 10

Agglutinative affixation and pro-drop, with the question particle -kah.

Malay 3 / 10

As Indonesian — suffix-building and dropped subjects.

Swahili 3 / 10

Builds words by stacking separable affixes exactly as Malayalam does, from an unrelated family on another continent.

Haitian Creole 0.5 / 10

Almost no overlap; included for completeness.
