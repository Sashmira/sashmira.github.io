Growing up Malayali in the Greater Toronto Area

Canada’s Malayali community is concentrated heavily around the GTA — Mississauga, Brampton, Scarborough — with strong pockets in Calgary and Vancouver. It is large enough to sustain Onam celebrations and church and temple communities, and yet, over and over, the children of those families end up understanding Malayalam without speaking it.

The reason is simple. A child hears Malayalam at home and Malayalam at community events, but is never in a situation where they must produce it. English carries school, friendships and screens. Understanding grows; speaking never gets exercised.

This is called receptive bilingualism, and reversing it does not need more exposure — your child already has plenty. It needs structured speaking practice with someone who will wait patiently for a Malayalam answer, which is very hard for a parent to do without frustration creeping in.
