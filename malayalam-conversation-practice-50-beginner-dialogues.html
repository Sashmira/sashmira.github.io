Malayalam conversation practice: 50 beginner dialogues

Fifty everyday situations, each with a model answer in Malayalam script and romanisation, grouped so you can practise ten a day.

How to use these 50 prompts

Each prompt below has a model answer in Malayalam script and romanisation. Cover the answer, try to say the prompt in Malayalam yourself, then check. Don't worry if your version differs a little: Malayalam often has several natural ways to say the same thing, and the model answer is simply the one I'd teach first.

Work through one group of ten a day. By the end of the week you will have said all fifty out loud at least once.

Make it a real conversation

  1. Pick three prompts from different groups and chain them: greet, ask a question, close politely.
  2. Record yourself, then listen for the long vowels (aa, ee, oo) and double consonants.
  3. Swap one word in each sentence: enikku chaaya venam becomes enikku kaappi venam (coffee).

The patterns behind these answers are explained in Malayalam sentence patterns for beginners and the question words guide. For live correction of your answers, book a lesson.
