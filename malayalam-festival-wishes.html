Malayalam Wishes for Birthdays, Anniversaries & Festivals

By Reshmi R Nair, Malayalam Linguist · Updated October 2026

Nothing delights a Malayali family like receiving wishes in Malayalam, especially from someone still learning. This guide gives you the greeting for every big occasion: birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, Onam, Vishu, Christmas, Eid, New Year and good luck before an exam. Each wish comes with the Malayalam script, an easy romanised spelling and the English meaning, ready for your next call or WhatsApp message.

The magic word: ashamsakal

Ashamsakal (ആശംസകൾ, also spelt aashamsakal) means “wishes” or “greetings.” Attach it to almost any occasion and you have a perfect greeting. Two words make any wish warmer: hridayam niranja (heartfelt, lit. “heart-filled”) and orupaadu (lots of).

Happy birthday

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
ജന്മദിനാശംസകൾJanmadinashamsakalHappy birthday
പിറന്നാൾ ആശംസകൾPirannaal ashamsakalHappy birthday (everyday)
ഒരുപാട് ഒരുപാട് ജന്മദിനാശംസകൾOrupaadu orupaadu janmadinashamsakalMany, many happy returns
ഹൃദയം നിറഞ്ഞ ജന്മദിനാശംസകൾHridayam niranja janmadinashamsakalHeartfelt birthday wishes
നന്മ വരട്ടെNanma varatteMay goodness come to you
ഈശ്വരൻ അനുഗ്രഹിക്കട്ടെEeshwaran anugrahikkatteMay God bless you

For children and close family, Malayalis often add a blessing after the wish. Janmadinashamsakal, mole! Nanma varatte. sounds completely native. Pirannaal also refers to the traditional birthday by the Malayalam star calendar, which many families still celebrate.

Happy anniversary and wedding wishes

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
വിവാഹ വാർഷിക ആശംസകൾVivaaha vaarshika ashamsakalHappy wedding anniversary
ഹൃദയം നിറഞ്ഞ വിവാഹ വാർഷിക ആശംസകൾHridayam niranja vivaaha vaarshika ashamsakalHeartfelt anniversary wishes
ഇനിയും ഒരുപാട് വർഷം സന്തോഷത്തോടെ ഒരുമിച്ച് ജീവിക്കട്ടെIniyum orupaadu varsham santhoshathode orumichu jeevikkatteMay you live happily together for many more years
വിവാഹ ആശംസകൾVivaaha ashamsakalWedding wishes / happy wedding
അഭിനന്ദനങ്ങൾAbhinandanangalCongratulations

Happy Onam

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
ഓണാശംസകൾOnashamsakalHappy Onam
എല്ലാവർക്കും എന്റെ ഹൃദയം നിറഞ്ഞ ഓണാശംസകൾEllavarkkum ente hridayam niranja OnashamsakalHeartfelt Onam wishes to everyone
ഓണസദ്യOna sadyaThe Onam feast
പൂക്കളംPookkalamFlower carpet

Onam (August–September) celebrates King Mahabali’s yearly visit: ten days of flower carpets, the grand Onam sadya feast and family reunions. One warm Onashamsakal in a family WhatsApp group earns instant affection. For more Onam words, see wedding and Onam vocabulary.

Happy Vishu

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
വിഷു ആശംസകൾVishu ashamsakalHappy Vishu
ഐശ്വര്യവും സമൃദ്ധിയും നിറഞ്ഞ വിഷു ആശംസകൾAishwaryavum samruddhiyum niranja Vishu ashamsakalWishing you a Vishu full of prosperity and plenty
വിഷുക്കണിVishukkaniThe first auspicious sight on Vishu morning
വിഷുക്കൈനീട്ടംVishukkaineettamVishu gift money from elders
കണി കണ്ടോ?Kani kando?Did you see the kani?

Vishu (mid-April) opens the Malayali new year. The night before, the family arranges the Vishukkani: golden konna flowers, rice, fruit, a lamp and a mirror. On waking, eyes stay closed until you reach the kani. Elders then give children Vishukkaineettam.

Merry Christmas

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
ക്രിസ്മസ് ആശംസകൾChristmas ashamsakalMerry Christmas
ക്രിസ്മസ് പുതുവത്സര ആശംസകൾChristmas puthuvalsara ashamsakalMerry Christmas and a happy New Year
നക്ഷത്രംNakshathramStar (the paper star lanterns hung outside Kerala homes)

Kerala’s Christian community celebrates with midnight mass, star lanterns, carol groups and plum cake. The English “Happy Christmas” is widely used too, but a Malayalam wish stands out.

Eid Mubarak

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
ഈദ് മുബാറക്Eid MubarakEid Mubarak (used as is)
പെരുന്നാൾ ആശംസകൾPerunnaal ashamsakalEid greetings (the Malayalam way)
ചെറിയ പെരുന്നാൾCheriya perunnaalEid al-Fitr (lit. small festival)
ബലി പെരുന്നാൾ / വലിയ പെരുന്നാൾBali perunnaal / Valiya perunnaalEid al-Adha (lit. big festival)

Perunnaal is the Malayalam word for Eid, so perunnaal ashamsakal is the natural local greeting alongside Eid Mubarak.

Happy New Year

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
പുതുവത്സരാശംസകൾPuthuvalsaraashamsakalHappy New Year
എല്ലാവർക്കും ഐശ്വര്യപൂർണ്ണമായ പുതുവത്സരാശംസകൾEllavarkkum aishwaryapoornamaaya puthuvalsaraashamsakalA prosperous New Year to everyone

Malayalis celebrate the 1 January New Year with this wish. The traditional Malayali new year is Vishu in April, and the Kollam calendar year begins in Chingam (August–September).

Good luck and all the best

MalayalamRomanisedEnglish
എല്ലാ ഭാവുകങ്ങളുംEllaa bhaavukangalumAll the best / best wishes
നന്നായി വരട്ടെNannaayi varatteMay it go well
വിജയിക്കട്ടെVijayikkatteMay you succeed
പരീക്ഷയ്ക്ക് ഓൾ ദി ബെസ്റ്റ്Pareekshakku all the bestAll the best for the exam

In daily speech, the English “all the best” is what most people say, often mixed into Malayalam as above. Ellaa bhaavukangalum is the warmer, more formal version for cards and messages.

Frequently asked questions

How do you say happy birthday in Malayalam?

“Janmadinashamsakal” (ജന്മദിനാശംസകൾ) or “pirannaal ashamsakal”. Add “nanma varatte” (may goodness come to you) to make it warmer.

How do you say happy anniversary in Malayalam?

“Vivaaha vaarshika ashamsakal” means happy wedding anniversary.

How do you wish Happy Onam in Malayalam?

Say “Onashamsakal!” For a fuller blessing: “Ellavarkkum ente hridayam niranja Onashamsakal”.

How do you say Happy Vishu in Malayalam?

“Vishu ashamsakal”. Vishu is the Malayali new year in mid-April.

How do you wish Eid in Malayalam?

“Perunnaal ashamsakal” is the Malayalam Eid greeting; “Eid Mubarak” is also widely used.

How do you say Happy New Year and Merry Christmas in Malayalam?

Happy New Year is “puthuvalsaraashamsakal” and Merry Christmas is “Christmas ashamsakal”.

How do you say good luck in Malayalam?

“Ellaa bhaavukangalum” (all the best) or “vijayikkatte” (may you succeed). In casual speech, “all the best” in English is common.
