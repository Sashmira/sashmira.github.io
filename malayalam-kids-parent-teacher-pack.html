Age routes

Ages 3 to 6

Five happy minutes: point, say, repeat, sing and use one phrase at home.

Ages 6 to 9

Read one page, act one dialogue, write one word and use one phrase with family.

Ages 9 to 13

Read a passage, answer questions, write a short paragraph and record a voice note.

Weekly routine

Day 1: hear and repeat. Day 2: point and name. Day 3: act it out. Day 4: draw or write. Day 5: call a relative and use one phrase.
